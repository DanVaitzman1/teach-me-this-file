Grandma's Sunday Pancakes

Ingredients (serves 4):
- 2 cups flour
- 2 eggs
- 1.5 cups milk
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- a pinch of salt

1. Mix the dry ingredients in a large bowl.
2. Whisk the eggs and milk, then pour them into the dry mix. Stir until just combined; a few lumps are fine.
3. Heat a lightly oiled pan over medium heat.
4. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake. Flip when bubbles appear on the surface, after about 2 minutes.
5. Cook 1 more minute and serve warm.
